The doctor

Dr Corina Rojas Gómez

Physician. I work in Alicante and the Valencian Community with a preventive and healthy-longevity focus.

My practice centres on supporting people who want to care for their health early: not only reacting when a problem appears, but understanding habits, history and useful markers to make informed decisions.

At Rojas.clinic I prioritise clarity: I explain in accessible language what we know, what we do not, and what makes most sense to try first. I avoid absolute promises; I prefer realistic, reviewable plans adapted to your life.

If you want a calm clinical conversation, close follow-up and no commercial pressure, that is how I work.

How I work

Listen first

Clinical history and personal context before proposing tests or changes.

Useful prevention

Prioritise what adds most value for your age, history and goals.

Honest follow-up

Review progress, adjust expectations and sustain habits with medical judgement.
