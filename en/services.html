Services

Prevention and longevity pathways

Three original Rojas.clinic pathways. None is a closed package: they adapt after the initial assessment.

Vitalumbral

Starting point

Initial review of habits, history and health priorities. Ideal if you want a clear first reading of where you are and what to improve first.

  • Assessment consultation
  • Priority report
  • Initial recommendations

Horizonte 360

Comprehensive programme

A broader prevention-oriented study: clinical review, guidance on lab work and a structured healthy-longevity plan with a follow-up calendar.

  • Extended assessment
  • Written personalised plan
  • Follow-up across several visits

Continuum

Ongoing support

For those who already have a plan and want to keep it alive: periodic adjustments, marker review and day-to-day consistency support.

  • Scheduled reviews
  • Goal adjustment
  • Follow-up channel

Pathway names are original to Rojas.clinic. Clinical content is defined in consultation for each person.

If you are unsure where to start, a first conversation is usually enough to point to the most useful pathway. Clinical detail (labs, reviews, calendar) is defined with you in consultation.
