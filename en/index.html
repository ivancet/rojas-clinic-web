The doctor

A careful look at your long-term health

I am Dr Corina Rojas Gómez, a physician. At Rojas.clinic I work with a preventive focus: understanding your history, habits and health markers to design a realistic plan you can sustain.

This is not about miracle promises, but informed clinical decisions, clear conversation and close support. The goal is simple: more years of life with quality, energy and autonomy.

Method

Three clear steps toward your longevity plan

An ordered process: first we listen and measure, then we design, and we always accompany you.

  1. 01

    Assessment

    Detailed clinical history, habit review and, when appropriate, studies aimed at prevention and early risk detection.

  2. 02

    Personalised plan

    Concrete priorities in nutrition, movement, sleep, stress and medical follow-up, adapted to your life and goals.

  3. 03

    Follow-up

    Periodic reviews to adjust the plan, measure progress and sustain motivation with clear clinical criteria.

Services

Prevention and longevity pathways

Original Rojas.clinic programmes. Each is adapted after the first assessment; they are not closed generic packages.

Vitalumbral

Starting point

Initial review of habits, history and health priorities. Ideal if you want a clear first reading of where you are and what to improve first.

  • Assessment consultation
  • Priority report
  • Initial recommendations

Horizonte 360

Comprehensive programme

A broader prevention-oriented study: clinical review, guidance on lab work and a structured healthy-longevity plan with a follow-up calendar.

  • Extended assessment
  • Written personalised plan
  • Follow-up across several visits

Continuum

Ongoing support

For those who already have a plan and want to keep it alive: periodic adjustments, marker review and day-to-day consistency support.

  • Scheduled reviews
  • Goal adjustment
  • Follow-up channel
