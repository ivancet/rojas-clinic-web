Privacy policy

This policy explains how personal data are processed at Rojas.clinic under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 on personal data protection and digital rights (LOPDGDD).

1. Data controller

Dr Corina Rojas Gómez. Email: corina@rojas.clinic. Locality: Alicante · Valencian Community (Spain). Professional address (for notifications): Avda. de Dénia, 47-A, 03013 Alicante (Colegio Oficial de Médicos de Alicante). Tax ID (NIF): 13442555K.

2. What data we process and why

The contact form on this site asks for your name, email address and message. These data are used only to answer your enquiry. The form does not store the information on the website: submitting it opens your email programme with a message addressed to corina@rojas.clinic. You can also write to that address directly. We do not ask for health data in the form; if you choose to include any in your message, they will be used only so we can reply.

3. Legal basis

The legal basis for this processing is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give by sending us the message. You may withdraw it at any time by writing to corina@rojas.clinic, without affecting the lawfulness of processing already carried out.

4. How long we keep the data

We keep the message for as long as needed to reply and, where appropriate, to follow up the request reasonably. If a professional relationship later arises, the data will also be kept for the applicable legal periods.

5. Recipients

We do not share or disclose these data to third parties for their own purposes, and we do not sell them. The message travels through the email service you use and the mailbox of corina@rojas.clinic. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may process technical data (such as your IP address) only to deliver the pages. That hosting does not receive the contents of the form.

6. Your rights

You may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and data portability by writing to corina@rojas.clinic and stating which right you wish to exercise. If you believe we have not handled your request properly, you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

7. Security and minors

We apply reasonable measures to protect information that reaches us by email. The practice’s services are aimed at adults. If you write on behalf of a minor, you must be entitled to do so.
