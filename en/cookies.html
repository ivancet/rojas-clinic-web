Cookie policy

Information under article 22.2 of the LSSI-CE about the cookies and third-party services this site actually uses. There are no maps, analytics tools, advertising or social-media pixels.

1. First-party storage (not an analytics or advertising cookie)

Rojas.clinic does not set its own analytics or advertising cookies. The site.js file only stores the following in your browser:

  • rojas_lang (local storage): the language you choose in the language switcher, so it can be remembered on later visits. This is a technical setting needed for the service you request.
  • rojas_lang_redirected (session storage): a temporary flag so the automatic language redirect on the Spanish home page is not repeated during the same visit.

You can delete this storage in your browser settings.

2. Third-party services the site does load

  • Fonts (Google Fonts): every page loads the Cormorant Garamond and Source Sans 3 typefaces from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com (Google). When you open the site, your browser connects to those servers to download the fonts and may send your IP address. We do not set a Google Fonts cookie ourselves; any processing is carried out by Google under its own policy.
  • Hosting (GitHub Pages): the pages are served by GitHub, Inc. It may process technical data (such as your IP address and access logs) in order to deliver the site. This is necessary for the pages to load.

The contact form does not set cookies: it opens your email programme. There are no embedded maps and no measurement scripts.

3. How to manage them

You can block or delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings. If we later add analytics or advertising cookies, we will ask for your consent before setting them and will update this policy.
